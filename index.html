🎯 REA Service Decision Flowchart

Complete workflow for exhibit assistance requests

Counsel asks you to help with exhibits
↓
STEP 1: Check Provider Dashboard
Go to Details → Job Info → Your Assigned Services
Is REA service listed?
↓
✓ YES - REA Ordered
REA Pre-Ordered Workflow
1 Before Depo: Check Job Files
  • Go to Details → Job Files
  • Download all exhibits, extract if zipped
  • Count exhibits
  • If NO exhibits: Post in #video-help
2 In Zoom: Upload to SC App
  • Upload to Unmarked Exhibits
3 Confirm REA with TA
"It looks like you've ordered Remote Exhibit Assistance for this proceeding. I want to confirm with you that I'll be marking and presenting your provided exhibits at your discretion today?"
4 Confirm Exhibit Count
"I have about [X] exhibits - does that sound correct to you?"
If count is wrong: Coordinate on how to receive missing exhibits
5 Ask Coordination Questions
  • "How will you refer to exhibits?" (A/B/C, 1/2/3, Bates, file names?)
  • "How should I know when to present?"
  • "Save annotated exhibits?"
6 Proceed
✓ Manage ALL exhibits as requested
✗ NO - REA Not Ordered
STEP 2: Count Exhibits Requested
How many exhibits does counsel need?
↓
1-2 Exhibits
Courtesy Service
1 Offer Courtesy
Up to 2 exhibits at no charge
2 Ask Questions
  • How will I receive them?
  • How will you refer to them?
3 Process
  • Download → Upload to SC App
  • Label → Present
⚠️ Stop at 2 maximum
3+ Exhibits
REA Service Required
1 Inform About Fee
"I'm happy to continue helping you share exhibits today, but just to let you know, there is an additional fee of up to $110/hour for this Remote Exhibit Assistant service. Would you like to proceed with that service?"
If TA Agrees:
  • Submit #video-help request
  • Wait for confirmation
  • Ask: How to receive? How to refer?
  • Receive → Upload → Label
  • Add worksheet note: "REA added mid-deposition"
  • Confirm readiness → Proceed
If TA Declines:
⚠️ Do NOT provide service
  • Acknowledge their position
  • Stop at 2 exhibits maximum
  • Only if TA pushes back on fee: Escalate in #video-help for guidance
